## CAMPAIGN SUMMARY

Sam Art Handicraft's Google Search Ads campaign will target users in India searching for handmade home decor and handicraft-related products.

The campaign will use the selected keywords identified through Google Keyword Planner. A Search campaign with one focused ad group will be used to keep the keywords, advertisements and landing page closely related.

The campaign is planned with a daily budget of ₹300 for 7 days, resulting in a planned total budget of ₹2,100.

The advertisements will highlight handmade home decor, unique handicrafts and the value of handcrafted products, with the website used as the final landing page.
